Preoptima — Identity direction · Round two

The idea you chose, without the crystal ball.

The team converged on one thing: the meaning behind the Palantir direction — a company that looks ahead, and helps people anticipate problems before the market sees them. What didn't land was the form.

So the idea stays and the orb goes. What follows answers the clearest ask in the feedback — a standalone icon, built from the P, that works with no wordmark at all — and keeps the foresight by making that P a bifurcation: the decision, and the alternative it hasn't foreclosed, held in a single shape.

01 / 02

The Preoptima P

A decision point — with the alternative still visible.

A single form arrives and, at the moment it would close, it divides. One path resolves into the bowl of the P; the other stays open as the void running through the letter.

That void is the idea. In most decisions the alternative disappears the moment you choose — here it stays legible, held inside the mark. It is a fork without a road: no arrows, no paths, no diagram. The divergence is built into the anatomy of the letter itself.

Drawn as one weight in a single ink, so it can carry the brand on its own — as an avatar, a favicon, an app tile, or anywhere the name is already understood.

One mark, three ways to use it

Horizontal, stacked, or the symbol standing alone.

The symbol was drawn first and the lockups follow from it, rather than the other way round — which is why it lifts out whole. Horizontal for the site header and documents; stacked where the space is square; the symbol on its own wherever the name is already obvious.

Alternative

The same idea, tilted forward

Direction and position, held in one form.

A P leaning into its own movement, where the counter resolves two ways at once: a flag rising, and a marker pointing down at a fixed point. The same anticipation, expressed as momentum rather than as a held-open alternative.

It reads faster than the primary mark and holds harder at very small sizes; it asks a little more of the viewer to resolve as a P. A different balance of the same ambition.

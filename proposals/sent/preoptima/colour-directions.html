Preoptima — Colour

Three options, two audiences.

These are not three preferences. Two of them are the architect’s register in different accents; the third is the planning officer’s. All three carry the same structure: a surface, a mid tone, an ink and a single accent, in a fixed proportion. All three clear WCAG AAA on every critical pair, so whichever you choose is accessible before it is anything else.

So the question comes in two parts: which register should the parent brand lead with —
and if it is the architect’s, which accent?

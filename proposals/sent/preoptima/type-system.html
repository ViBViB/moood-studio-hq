Preoptima — Typography

Three faces, three jobs.

These are not three preferences. They are three jobs — the headline, the reading text, and the number. Cal Sans sets the headlines — a deliberate sibling to the logotype, sharing its geometric construction and round bowls without imitating its letters. Manrope carries everything else: reading text, interface labels, captions. IBM Plex Mono is reserved for measured data. All three are open-licence and self-hostable — no licence cost, and nothing billed per seat when they enter the product.
